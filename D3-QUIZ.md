# D3 Quiz — One-question-at-a-time practice

This quiz is aligned to the D3 study domain (20% exam weight). It covers task statements 3.1–3.6 and the common traps listed in the domain file.

## How to use
- Answer the question displayed in chat; only one question is shown at a time.
- After submission, receive correctness, the correct answer, rationale, a misconception/escalation note, and an offer of deeper explanation.
- Say **pause** to stop at a checkpoint; say **resume** to continue from the next unanswered question.
- Say **explain more** after feedback for a deeper explanation.
- Each submitted answer is appended to `testresultscratchpad.md`. The file records attempts and weaknesses for follow-up training.

## Question bank
The machine-readable bank is in [d3-quiz.json](./d3-quiz.json).
